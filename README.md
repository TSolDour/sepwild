# SEP-WILD - funded by region Nouvelle-Aquitaine

## AIM

Comparative approach to understand the influence of nursery quality (measured metal burden, pH data from survey networks) on the DNA methylation status, transcriptional response and overall growth rate of cuttlefish juveniles.

This project was designed based on the results of the RNA-sep2 project (paper in prep.).

## Methodology

About **59 cuttlefish juveniles** have been sampled in July 2026 at Oleron island (July 15th & 16th, n=29) and in the Arcachon basin (July 17th, n=30). Tissues expected to serve for nucleic acid extraction were fast frozen in liquid nitrogen, then stored at -80°C. Other tissues were stored at -20°C.

Analyses :
	- **Colorimetric measurement of total DNA methylation** in optic lobes, gills and muscle; 
	- **Metal concentration** measurement in digestive gland; 
	- **Ageing using statoliths**; 
	- **Transcript expression quantification** by RT-qPCR of some genes of interest (SOD, GST, GS, mGluR3, DNMT1, DNMT3).

## FAIR and reproducibility

- A **Data Management Plan** (DMP) has been made and will be implemented throughout the research process to anticipate every part of data life cycle and ensure to comply with FAIR principles. 

- All numerical analyses will be performed in R using specific portability and reproducibility tools : *renv*, *tragets*.
